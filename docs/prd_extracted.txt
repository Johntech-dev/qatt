1. Overview
The Student Attendance Management System is a web application that replaces manual, paper-based attendance with a QR code based digital process. A lecturer generates a unique QR code for each lecture session; students scan the code with their smartphone browser to mark their attendance instantly. The system stores attendance records in a database and gives lecturers and administrators real-time reports.
2. Problem Statement
Manual roll calls and sign-in sheets waste lecture time.
Students can sign in for absent classmates (proxy attendance).
Paper registers are easily lost, damaged, or hard to total up.
Lecturers have no real-time view of attendance trends.
3. Goals
Let lecturers generate a unique, time-bound QR code per session.
Let students mark attendance in seconds by scanning the code.
Prevent proxy attendance through session-specific, expiring codes.
Give lecturers and admins real-time, exportable attendance reports.
Work on any device with a web browser — no app installation required.
4. Users & Roles
Administrator
Manages student records, courses, and lecturer accounts.
Lecturer
Creates/owns courses, approves or auto-enrolls students, generates QR codes, views attendance reports for their own courses.
Student
Registers/enrolls into a lecturer's course, then scans QR codes to mark attendance for that course only.

6. User Flow
6.1 One-Time Setup: Course Enrollment
Lecturer logs in → creates a course (e.g. CSC 301 – Web Development).
Student logs in → browses/searches available courses → registers/enrolls in the lecturer's course.
Lecturer optionally reviews and approves the enrollment (or it's automatic, based on settings).
Once enrolled, the student is permanently linked to that course for the semester.
6.2 Recurring Flow: Taking Attendance
Lecturer logs in → selects one of their courses → generates a session QR code.
QR code is displayed on a projector or shared screen in class.
Student opens the app → scans the QR code with their phone.
System checks that the student is enrolled in that course, then validates the code and session window → records attendance.
A student who is not enrolled in the course cannot mark attendance for it, even with the QR code.
Lecturer/admin views real-time attendance report for that session.

8. Non-Functional Requirements
Performance: attendance scan-to-save should complete in under 2 seconds.
Usability: no app installation — works in any mobile browser.
Security: QR codes are session-specific and time-bound; only enrolled students can mark attendance for a given course.
Reliability: attendance data must persist accurately with no duplicate records.
Scalability: should support multiple concurrent scans per session.
9. Out of Scope (v1)
Biometric or facial recognition verification.
Native mobile app (iOS/Android).
Integration with national ID or third-party school management systems.
Geolocation-based attendance validation (future enhancement).
10. Success Metrics
Attendance capture time reduced from minutes to under 10 seconds per class.
Zero recorded cases of proxy attendance during testing.
100% of test attendance records accurately stored and retrievable.
System successfully demonstrated on live devices during defense.
